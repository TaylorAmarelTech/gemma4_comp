Process Files -- bulk exploration of a case bundle

Trust boundary: Uploaded rows are processed locally in the kernel (/kaggle/working/process-staging/). The harness runs in-process; no row is sent to any remote service. Outputs stay in the kernel until you explicitly Anonymize + Submit.

Drop a ZIP, folder, CSV, or JSONL of case material -- chat exports, scans, recruitment ads, court excerpts, recorded testimony transcripts. Each row goes through the GREP harness (161 rules) and a regex entity extractor (AMOUNT / PHONE / EMAIL / STATUTE / CORRIDOR). The kernel builds a local privacy-aware knowledge graph from the extracted entities and indicators. Then ask Gemma 4 questions about the bundle.

What happens when you upload

Step 1
Unpack
ZIP/folder unpacked into /kaggle/working/process-staging/. Files counted, types detected.
Step 2
Extract
Regex extractor pulls AMOUNT / PHONE / EMAIL / STATUTE / CORRIDOR. GREP scans each row for 161 trafficking indicators.
Step 3
Graph
Entities link into a local graph: people, employers, fees, corridors, statutes. Nothing leaves the kernel.
Step 4
Chat
Ask Gemma 4 questions about the bundle. Answers cite specific extracted facts + GREP rule hits.

Typical use: an NGO uploads a folder of recruitment chats and scans. The graph surfaces fee outliers, corridor patterns, and document-retention signals. The chat answers "Which messages mention placement fees over the legal cap?" with direct row pointers.
