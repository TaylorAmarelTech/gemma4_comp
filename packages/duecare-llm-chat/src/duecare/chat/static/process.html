Bulk File Review

LOCAL NO REMOTE CALLS REVIEWER GATED Rows stay in /kaggle/working/process-staging/ until you explicitly export or share.

Drop a ZIP, CSV, JSONL, Office document, PDF, image, email, or text bundle of case material: chat exports, scans, recruitment ads, court excerpts, recorded testimony transcripts, and document photos. Each row goes through the process harness: GREP, entity extraction, document typing, person linking, hierarchy construction, and a deterministic case brief. With Standard review (the default), the upload job ALSO runs three Gemma 4 passes inline — a text case-brief pass, a typed-edge + RAG synthesis pass, and a contextual media-review pass over each queued asset — capped by the Max Gemma calls budget. The Re-run Gemma 4 edge pass button in Step 3 lets you re-trigger the typed-edge synthesis after edits or after importing a new local knowledge pack. When a fine-tuned Gemma 4 adapter trained on reviewed document-classification and graph-edge examples is loaded, it replaces the base model and improves page-item routing, edge typing, and bulk-file edge quality.

What this workflow does

This page is a local document-review workbench. Upload a case folder, let the process harness parse and link the evidence, review the extracted intelligence, confirm it, then ask focused investigative questions or export the bundle for the appendix notebooks.

Processing configuration

Default: Standard review. Deterministic extraction runs first; Gemma 4 is budgeted for high-signal page items and knowledge candidates.
recommended default selected
Where Gemma 4 runs on this page (current defaults):
  1. Upload job — Gemma 4 text case brief — the upload job sends the bundle summary to Gemma 4 and gets back a 2-paragraph case brief. 1 call.
  2. Upload job — Gemma 4 typed-edge + RAG synthesis — the upload job sends the seed graph to Gemma 4 to extract additional typed edges (charged_or_collected_fee, fee_camouflage_evidence, document_control_signal, etc.) and RAG candidates. 1 call.
  3. Upload job — Gemma 4 contextual media review — for each queued image / scan / PDF, the upload job sends Gemma 4 the filename, folder, linked case, and prepared review questions; Gemma predicts proposed_edges. Up to (Max Gemma calls − 2) per upload.
  4. Step 3 button — Re-run Gemma 4 edge pass — optional. Re-runs the edge synthesis on the updated graph after the reviewer adjusts or imports new knowledge.
  5. Step 4 graph chat — deterministic graph analyst answers from the cached graph, then a short Gemma 4 synthesis adds a 2-3 sentence conversational read. Route badge above each answer shows which path fired.
Standard review (default) sets Max Gemma calls to 10, runs all three upload-job passes inline, then enables the graph-chat synthesis. Quick triage skips the upload-job passes; Exhaustive review increases the cap.
Model fit: Deterministic extraction works without Gemma. Smaller text models are best for text-grounded edge proposals and graph chat. OCR quality depends on local OCR, and image/audio/video edge extraction requires local preprocessing plus a multimodal-capable model/runtime.

Advanced processing plan

Bulk review runs four stacked passes: (1) deterministic inventory + chunking + GREP + entity extraction; (2) Gemma 4 text case-brief; (3) Gemma 4 typed-edge + RAG synthesis; (4) Gemma 4 contextual media review per queued asset (filename + folder + linked-case context to predict edges). Pixel-level OCR/vision is intentionally still queued — when the local OCR engine + Gemma 4 multimodal processor are wired, the contextual prediction is replaced by direct image inspection.
