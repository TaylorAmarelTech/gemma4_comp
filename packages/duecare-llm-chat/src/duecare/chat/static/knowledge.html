Knowledge Extraction

Trust boundary: Extraction runs locally against the loaded Gemma 4 model. Drafted envelopes stay in /kaggle/working/knowledge/; nothing leaves the kernel unless you anonymize and submit on Anonymization & Sharing.

Paste raw text or upload a source bundle from recruiter messages, case notes, statutes, reports, PDFs, images, or contracts. The extraction harness anonymizes it, scans it against existing GREP/RAG knowledge, asks Gemma 4 to draft useful knowledge objects, and shows suggested envelopes for review. Advanced users can still choose a specific leaf type or manually author any taxonomy leaf.

What this workflow does

This page turns reviewed text and uploaded source bundles into maintainable knowledge objects. The normal path is add source, draft suggestions, promote useful envelopes, then finish by exporting or sharing through the redaction flow.

No schema up frontGemma 4 and deterministic hints suggest useful object types automatically.
Reviewer controlledDrafts are suggestions until a human promotes them into the local knowledge store.
Pack readyPromoted objects can be exported, synced, or anonymized for hub submission.
