Knowledge Extraction

Trust boundary: Extraction runs locally against the loaded Gemma 4 model. Drafted envelopes stay in /kaggle/working/knowledge/; nothing leaves the kernel unless you anonymize and submit on Anonymization & Sharing.

Paste raw text from a recruiter message, case note, statute, report, or contract. The extraction harness anonymizes it, scans it against existing GREP/RAG knowledge, asks Gemma 4 to draft useful knowledge objects, and shows suggested envelopes for review. Advanced users can still choose a specific leaf type or manually author any taxonomy leaf.

1 Add source text Paste a passage or load an example. The normal path does not require choosing a schema first. Ready

3 Import, export, or share knowledge packs Round-trip the sample bundle, promote useful objects, or open anonymized sharing. Waiting
