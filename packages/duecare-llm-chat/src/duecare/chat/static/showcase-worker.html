Showcase · For workers checking a message privately

Check a suspicious offer or contract before you act on it.

Migrant workers and prospective workers can use the workbench to privately check job offers, contracts, recruiter messages, and fee demands. The on-device Gemma model + the harness explain warning signs in plain language and point to the right hotline for your corridor — without sending your message to a remote server.

Privacy boundary: messages typed here stay on the kernel running this URL. Nothing is sent to the public hub unless you explicitly use the Anonymize page and click submit.
