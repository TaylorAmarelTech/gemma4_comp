Important: The Duecare harness never auto-sends reports on a user's behalf. Click-to-call (tel:) and click-to-email (mailto:) links open your device's phone app or mail client with pre-filled context — you review and submit. Web-form URLs open in a new tab. Auto-sending would create abuse vectors and remove worker agency over the report.
