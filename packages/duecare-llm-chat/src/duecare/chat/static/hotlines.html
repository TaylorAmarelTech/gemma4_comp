Important: The DueCare harness never auto-sends reports on a user's behalf. Click-to-call (tel:) and click-to-email (mailto:) links open your device's phone app or mail client with pre-filled context. You review and submit. Web-form URLs open in a new tab. Auto-sending would create abuse vectors and remove worker agency over the report. Contacts are volatile knowledge-pack data: verify the shown last updated value before relying on a phone number, email, or form. If a contact is wrong, please file an issue at github.com/TaylorAmarelTech/gemma4_comp with the contact id and the corrected information.
