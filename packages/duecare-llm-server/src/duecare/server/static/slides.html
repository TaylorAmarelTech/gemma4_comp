DueCare / Gemma 4 Good

Safety infrastructure for migrant-worker protection.

DueCare uses local Gemma 4 plus deterministic harnesses to moderate risky content, analyze case files, support offline-capable worker guidance, power research, and share anonymized knowledge.

Problem introduction

Exploitation continues because the protective workflow is fragmented.

Recruitment abuse, document retention, debt bondage, and retaliation are cross-border systems. The response is often split across moderation queues, paper files, phone advice, and slow research.

1

Unsafe content gets through

Recruitment ads and messages camouflage fees as training, deposits, or salary assignments.

2

Case analysis is slow

Chats, receipts, contracts, scans, and complaints rarely become a linked evidence graph.

3

Workers need private answers

Rights information must work in phone-first, low-bandwidth, and offline-capable local workflows.

4

Research is under-powered

Patterns across agencies, accounts, routes, and remedies are buried in unstructured files.

The deck frames the scale of exploitation and the system gaps. Individual stories appear only inside use-case demos.
Solution introduction

Five comparable workflows, one shared local substrate.

The product lanes are user-facing workflows. Gemma 4 is not another lane; it is the local model runtime underneath the same evidence, safety, and review controls.

Information access overview

Workers need clear answers in the channels they can actually use.

The phone-facing experience is built for short questions, plain-language rights, safe next steps, and local evidence packs.

Plain language

No legal maze before the direct answer.

Offline-capable

Local Gemma 4 and bundled corridor packs can run without cloud APIs when the selected model build is available.

Safety aware

Preserve evidence, avoid retaliation escalation, and route to trusted support.

Privacy aware

Do not force workers to disclose more than the answer requires.

DueCareoffline
My employer says they need to keep my passport. I am afraid to complain.
You should be able to keep your passport. If asking directly feels unsafe, save messages, contact a trusted NGO or consulate, and avoid warning the employer before you have support.
Can I report without losing my job?
Retaliation may be prohibited, but pressure can still happen. Use a safe reporting channel and keep copies of contracts, receipts, and messages.

Demo: the cached worker question loads directly into the slide.

Audience: worker / Use case: PH-HK placement feecached
A recruiter says I have to pay PHP 50,000 in training fees before my Hong Kong visa will be released. Is this legal?
Local response
No. For Filipino domestic workers bound for Hong Kong, the worker-paid placement fee should be zero PHP under the corridor rule. Training, processing, medical, documentation, or transport charges can still be prohibited if they function as recruitment costs. Safer next steps: - Do not pay until you have checked the agency and fee rule. - Keep screenshots, receipts, messages, and account numbers. - Ask for a written breakdown of each charge and who receives it. - Contact the relevant labour authority, consulate, or vetted NGO before confronting the recruiter if you fear retaliation. Research overview

Research questions become graph questions, not spreadsheet archaeology.

A researcher or regulator can ask across reviewed evidence: which agencies recur, which bank accounts receive fees, which corridors show provider-choice restrictions, and where refunds or claims succeeded.

Signals

Grouping

Evidence

Anonymized knowledge sharing

Reviewed case facts can improve the system without exposing the worker.

The goal is not to upload raw case files. The goal is to promote small, verified facts with privacy metadata, evidence provenance, and de-identification status.

Knowledge facts

Fee paid, refund obtained, complaint filed, clinic required, account observed, contract clause disputed.

Privacy gate

Remove names, phone numbers, exact addresses, documents, free-text identifiers, and unnecessary dates.

Server value

Shared objects improve retrieval packs, research, moderation rules, and future edge generation.

FAQ

Likely judge questions.

Does it require cloud APIs?

No. The core workbench runs locally. Optional online tools are explicit, specialized, and off by default.

Why not just prompt Gemma?

The harness adds rules, citations, tools, privacy gates, and grading. That is what makes behavior inspectable.

What happens to uploaded case files?

They stay in local process staging until the reviewer explicitly exports or shares anonymized objects.

How is accuracy checked?

Rulegrade dimensions, optional LLM judge passes, row citations, and reviewer confirmation all create accountability.
