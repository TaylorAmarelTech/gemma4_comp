Solution introduction

Six comparable workflows, one shared local substrate.

Exploitation continues because the protective workflow is fragmented. Six user-facing lanes sit on top of the same evidence, safety, and review controls. Gemma 4 is not another lane — it is the local model runtime underneath.

Workflow lane What the user does What DueCare returns
Content moderation Review ads, messages, listings, and harmful compliance requests. Risk labels, fired indicators, policy-grounded refusal or escalation notes.
Case analysis Upload a bounded case bundle and confirm extracted facts. People, payments, dates, typed edges, graph chat, complaint drafts.

Research and enforcement Ask cross-case questions about agencies, accounts, corridors, remedies. Clusters, evidence rows, uncertainty notes, verification checklists.

Retaliation may be prohibited but pressure can still happen. Use a safe reporting channel and keep copies of contracts, receipts, and messages.

A worker question, answered.

A recruiter says I have to pay PHP 50,000 in training fees before my Hong Kong visa will be released. Is this legal?
No. For Filipino domestic workers bound for Hong Kong, the worker-paid placement fee should be zero PHP under the corridor rule. Training, processing, medical, and transport charges can still be prohibited if they function as recruitment costs.

FAQ

Frequently asked.

Does it require cloud APIs?

No. The workbench runs locally. Optional online tools are explicit, specialized, and off by default.

Why not just prompt Gemma?

The harness adds rules, citations, tools, sensitive-data handling, and grading. That is what makes Gemma 4 behavior inspectable and safe to put in front of a worker or regulator.

What happens to uploaded files?

They stay in local process staging until the reviewer explicitly exports a report or shares anonymized knowledge objects.

How is accuracy benchmarked?

A-00 runs base, harnessed, fine-tuned, and fine-tuned+harness arms on the same prompts. Rulegrade and optional LLM judge produce comparable scores.

What model sizes are supported?

Gemma 4 E2B for fast demos. E4B and E9B for higher-quality runs. Quantized 4-bit by default to fit Kaggle T4.

How do harness updates reach the workbench?

GREP rules and knowledge packs are versioned JSON bundles. Synced from the hub or imported manually. Always reviewed before live deployment.
