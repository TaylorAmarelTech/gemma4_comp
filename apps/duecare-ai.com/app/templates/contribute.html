Improve the public knowledge. Keep cases out.

All contributions are public-source proposals. They start as proposed, go through PII scan and curator review, and only then become vetted packs. The form below will block obvious case content before submission.

Review pipeline

Submit → scan → review → sign → sync.

Step 01
Submit proposal

Public-source URL, summary, jurisdiction. No raw case content.

Step 02 · boundary
Automated PII scan

Edge filter rejects emails, phones, ID-like strings, and free-text case content.

Step 03
Curator review

Signed-in curator inspects the proposed diff against the public source.

Step 04
Vetted pack

Curator signs; checksum updated; manifest references previous version.

Step 05
Client sync

Local deployments pull the new signed version on next sync.

PII scanner

Edge filter rules

Submissions are auto-rejected if they contain:

  • Email or phone-number patterns in free text
  • ID-like strings (passport, NID, work-permit)
  • Likely worker / employer names paired with case verbs
  • Free-text fields longer than 800 characters
