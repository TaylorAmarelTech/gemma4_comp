privacy is non-negotiable

The hub does not receive raw cases.

This page is the contract. If anything Duecare ships ever appears to violate the rules below, treat it as a bug report. The boundary is enforced in code, not in copy.

Hard rule 01

No raw case intake.

The hub has no endpoint that accepts worker case content. None will be added.

Hard rule 02

No auto-reporting.

Duecare drafts; the user or trusted caseworker decides. The hub does not send messages on anyone's behalf.

Hard rule 03

No legal counsel.

Duecare is not a law firm, an emergency response service, or an official complaint authority. It cites public sources and routes to verified contacts.

Hard rule 04

No surprise telemetry.

Aggregate signals are opt-in per partner and per turn. The default for new deployments is off.
