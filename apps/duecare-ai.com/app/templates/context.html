Corridor & jurisdiction RAG

Knowledge moves. Cases don't.

Context packs are signed, versioned bundles of public legal and regulatory material. organized by corridor and jurisdiction. They're what gets shipped back to local Duecare runs so every grounded answer cites a verifiable source.

01 · Pack lifecycle

Public source → Sentinel proposal → Curator review → Vetted pack.

Every snippet that ends up in a corridor pack came from a public source, was proposed as a diff by Sentinel, was reviewed by a human curator, and was signed before distribution.

Public source step · 01

Worker-paid recruitment fees and related costs are prohibited under the receiving-employer-pays principle. The receiving employer in Qatar is responsible for all costs of recruitment, including agency commission, medical exams, and travel. This applies regardless of any pre-existing arrangement between the worker and the recruitment agency in the sending country. — gov.qa labor ministry circular 2026-04 · ilo C181 art. 7
