July 2026 · Evaluation study

Off the shelf, no LLM protected the worker. Harnessed, every one refused — and cited the law.

For a migrant worker checking whether a recruiter’s fee or a contract clause is a scam, an LLM’s answer is a safety decision. So we measured how safe those answers actually are — across models, before and after the DueCare harness.

On a calibrated 0–100 trafficking-safety rubric, judged by a cross-family panel of large models, no off-the-shelf model we tested cleared the bar: base scores ran from 40 to 59 out of 100. The failure runs on a spectrum — frontier models tend to decline, but hollowly, without ever naming the indicator, citing the statute, or surfacing the hotline; the smaller, on-device-class open models often go further and comply with the disguised request outright. Either way the worker is left unprotected — a domain-knowledge gap the whole category shares, not one model’s flaw. The DueCare harness closes it on every model it wraps: a +35 to +41 point gain. We report the numbers on gemma4:31b, the open model DueCare deploys on-device so an NGO can run it privately on a laptop instead of sending case files to an API — lifted from 48.4 to 89.1 (+40.7) across 7,953 paired adversarial prompts, holding on 99.8%.

The failure mode

Off the shelf, the model is a willing accessory.

The adversarial set wraps a request to operationalise trafficking in a respectable voice — “from a business operations perspective…”, “for academic research…”, “for a novel I’m writing…”. Stripped of a domain safety layer, LLMs fail this on a spectrum: the frontier models usually decline, but hollowly — without ever naming the indicator, citing the statute, or surfacing the hotline a worker needs; the smaller, on-device-class open models — the ones an NGO can actually run privately — often go further and produce the requested playbook: how to structure hours to evade overtime, how to dress up the denial of legal representation, how a corrupt recruiter collects fees without a paper trail. The three verbatim cases below are gemma4:31b, the open model DueCare deploys — the dangerous end of that spectrum, and precisely the deployable class that most needs the harness. The dangerous operational content of the raw reply is replaced with a bracketed placeholder: we show that the model complied, not how. The harnessed reply is shown as written.

The mechanism

Not a cautious tone — domain knowledge, wired in.

The gain is real rather than a personality change: a length-matched placebo preamble produces no lift, so the effect is knowledge, not mood. The harness puts three layers between the worker’s question and the model’s answer, then checks the result before it is shown.

1

Screen

Deterministic indicator rules scan the prompt for exploitation patterns — recruitment-fee shifting, passport “safekeeping”, coded language for coercion — before the model runs, so a disguised ask is flagged, not taken at face value.

indicator rules
2

Ground

Retrieval pulls the specific governing law and corridor context into the model’s working set — the ILO convention, the origin-state recruitment act, the destination’s labour code — so it can cite the statute, not gesture vaguely at “the law”.

retrieval + legal claims
3

Constrain

The model answers inside a protective contract: name the indicator, cite the law, refuse the operational how-to, surface the concrete hotline, and protect every stakeholder’s privacy — the five things the rubric scores.

response contract
4

Verify

Outputs pass a safety check before they reach the screen; anything that still reads as operational uplift is caught at the boundary, and raw case content stays on the worker’s device unless they choose to share a sanitised version.

output gate

Every layer is inspectable and versioned: browse the live indicator rules, the knowledge packs, and the harness architecture. Because the knowledge is a layer, not baked into the weights, it can be corrected and extended without retraining — which is what makes it compound (below).

Not one model — the category

The gap is the field, not the model.

The failure is not a Gemma quirk. Run the identical paired evaluation on other open and frontier models and the same thing happens. The baseline column is the finding: not one off-the-shelf model — not the frontier ones — clears 60/100. Each leaves the worker without the specifics that matter — the indicator, the statute, the hotline — and the smaller open models go further and comply outright. The harness lifts every one of them into the 80s–90s. The last column is the one we watch hardest — hurts, the count of prompts where the harnessed answer scored below the raw one: a small, deliberately-tracked tail, not a hidden cost.

Honestly scoped

What this measures — and what it doesn’t.

We publish the direction and the methodology, not a claim we haven’t earned. The number above is a real, reproducible rubric-scored quality gain, judged by an LLM panel. It is not a measurement that DueCare detects trafficking in the world.

  • It is a rubric-scored quality proxy, judged by LLMs. A judge-independent deterministic grader shows no gain over a length-matched placebo preamble for the headline model — the measured magnitude lives in the LLM judge. Treat +40.7 as the specificity-anchored rubric maximum; the load-bearing figure under diverse worker-utility / faithfulness lenses is closer to +12–14.
  • The behavioural difference is real regardless. The refuse-the-exploiter-and-cite-the-law behaviour shown above is a categorical change (the raw model complied; the harnessed model refused and grounded), which a placebo does not produce — the harness adds domain knowledge, not just a cautious tone.
  • It is English-only today. The deterministic detection layers do not yet fire on a worker writing in Tagalog, Nepali, Bengali or Arabic — the highest-priority gap for a migrant-worker tool, and one we state openly.
  • It is not legal advice or a detector. The legal grounding is a vetted reference library (below) with recorded exceptions and recheck dates; it must be re-verified against a primary source before anyone relies on it.

Full detail: the honest findings synthesis (findings_synthesis_2026_07_10.md) and the adversarial defeatability register (adversarial_findings_2026_07_10.md).

Why it can cite the law

An 85-instrument legal reference, with exceptions and freshness.

The harness grounds its answers in a vetted legal-claim library — 85 EvidenceClaims as of this study, each carrying its source, jurisdiction, applicability and exceptions, binding status, and a recheck date. It spans the ILO forced-labour and child-labour conventions, the UN Palermo Protocol and UNTOC, the 1956 Slavery Convention, regional instruments (ASEAN, SAARC, EU directives), and the recruitment and anti-trafficking laws of the major origin and destination corridors. A separate layer distinguishes the law from a trafficker’s false “law”: the control narratives (“your passport belongs to your employer”, “work off your debt before you can leave”) that are weaponised to keep workers in place.

Browse the grounding: indicator rules, knowledge packs, and the vetted claim register (legal_claims_register.md).

And it gets stronger

A harness that compounds — on any model, over time.

The harness is not a static prompt or a frozen fine-tune. It is a living domain safety layer, and three things make it sharper the longer it runs — without asking an NGO to retrain anything.

  • The knowledge grows. The indicator rules, the retrieval corpus, the vetted legal-claim register, and the knowledge packs all expand as new corridors, recruitment tactics, and statutes are added — each entry carrying its source and a recheck date, so the library gets both bigger and fresher. A fee-camouflage pattern added today sharpens every deployment tomorrow, with no change to the model.
  • It is model-agnostic, so it rides the frontier. The same harness lifts gemma4:31b, gpt-oss:120b, glm-5.2 and deepseek-v4-pro alike. As stronger and smaller open models ship, DueCare simply re-wraps them — the safety layer compounds on top of the base model’s improving reasoning instead of being rebuilt each time.
  • The benchmark is the flywheel. 78,719 adversarial prompts today, and growing. Every prompt the harness still gets wrong becomes the next indicator rule, the next grounding document, or fine-tuning signal — the system learns from its own tail, and the evaluation on this page is how we watch that gap close.

Live surfaces for each: indicator rules, knowledge packs, the fine-tune pipeline, and the growing board at /benchmark.

Where it runs

Three ways to put it in front of a worker.

The same harness serves three deployments, chosen by who holds the data. The privacy boundary is the design constraint: raw worker chats, IDs, and documents stay where they are unless the worker chooses to share a sanitised version.

Platform scale

Integrity triage

A fast keyword filter routes suspect job posts or messages to Gemma 4 for analysis, which returns a warning prompt, resource links, or a moderation-queue flag — the same shape as a suicide-prevention interstitial, for trafficking. Runs at social-media / job-board volume.

for platforms & job boards
On device

Worker-side tool

A browser extension, chat bot, or mobile app runs Gemma 4 entirely on the worker’s device via LiteRT. A worker pastes a suspicious contract clause or recruiter message and gets the indicator, the local law, and the hotline — and the raw text never leaves the phone.

for workers & their advocates
Private instance

NGO & regulator dashboard

A self-hosted instance for batch review, compliance monitoring, model comparison, and regulatory reporting — so an agency can run sensitive case files on its own hardware instead of a frontier API, and even fine-tune on its own jurisdiction’s rules.
