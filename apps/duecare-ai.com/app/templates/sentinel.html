Public-source crawl proposals

Propose a public-source crawl. Curators review the diff.

The public information research monitor turns a public URL into a proposed pack diff. It does not auto-publish, does not auto-merge, and never crawls private endpoints. Every output is a draft for human review.

Prototype Draft only · curator must sign Public URLs only
Crawl pipeline

URL → fetch → extract facts → diff → curator.

01
Public source

Government advisory, ILO/HRW publication, or NGO public report.

02
Crawl proposal

Submitter records URL, jurisdiction, pack kind, and reason.

03
Extract public facts

The monitor pulls a small set of structured facts and a content hash.

04
Proposed diff

Diff is queued against the current pack version.

05 · gate
Curator review

A signed-in curator inspects diff vs source. Only vetted packs sync to clients.

Submit a crawl request

The monitor only fetches publicly reachable URLs. It does not log in, scrape rate-limited APIs, or follow links across domains.

Must be publicly reachable. The monitor fetches once and stores a content hash.
Hard limits

What the monitor will not do

  • Crawl behind logins or rate-limits
  • Follow links across domains
  • Auto-publish without curator signature
  • Accept any worker, employer, or platform-user content
