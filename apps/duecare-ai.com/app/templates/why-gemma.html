Why DueCare runs on Gemma 4.

DueCare is shared infrastructure that has to run cheaply at NGOs and on workers’ phones, behave well in many corridors and many languages, and be inspectable end-to-end. Gemma 4 is the only family that lets us hit all four bars at once.

01 · Five reasons

Why this family, specifically.

Each reason maps to something concrete a partner or worker actually feels.

Reason 01

Open weights are a privacy primitive

Open weights mean an NGO can run DueCare on its own server. A worker can run the small variant on their own phone. Closed APIs cannot do this. With Gemma 4, raw casework never has to leave the building, and worker chats never have to leave the device. The privacy boundary stops being a promise we make and becomes a fact you can verify by reading the deployment.

Reason 02

Cheap inference makes the math work

Per-token cost on Gemma 4 is roughly an order of magnitude cheaper than equivalently-tuned closed APIs at the sizes DueCare uses. Most users of DueCare are NGOs and labour-ministry teams — their tooling budget is small. Cheap inference is what makes “run the harness on every job post” or “answer every worker question with a cited draft” affordable in real life.

Reason 03

Native tool calling, not bolt-on

Gemma 4 emits a structured tool-call format out of the base model. The harness uses it directly — it is how grep-rule outputs, knowledge-pack lookups, and license-registry checks get composed into a single answer. That removes a whole category of brittle prompt-engineering between the model and the tools.

Reason 04

Open weights are fine-tunable

The harness ships base packs for general behaviour and per-corridor LoRA adapters for the language and idioms a corridor actually uses. A closed API would force us to ship the union of all corridors to every user. With Gemma 4 we can ship a small Tagalog-domestic adapter to PHL→KWT and a Bengali-construction adapter to BGD→SAU, on top of the same shared base.

Reason 05

On-device is the worker chat unlock

The 2B-class quantized variant runs at conversational speed on a low-spec phone, without a network. That makes the worker app possible at all: a labourer at a recruitment office can paste a contract clause into the app, get a cited draft response, and never upload a single byte. Without an open-weights, on-device-capable model, this product cannot exist in the form we believe in.

Reason 06

Inspectable end-to-end

Researchers can reproduce our evals against the same weights we ran. They can replay an audit feed, pull the same pack version, run it through the same model, and see whether the answer matches. With a closed API, “reproducible” degrades to “snapshot of someone else’s output”.

03 · What DueCare adds on top

The base model is a starting point, not the product.

Gemma 4 is the substrate. The harness, knowledge packs, tools, evals, and corridor adapters are what make it useful.

Layer 01

Knowledge packs

Signed corridor knowledge with public-source citations. The model never claims a fact a pack can’t cite.

Layer 02

Grep rules

Cheap deterministic detectors that run before the model. Catch obvious patterns without an inference call.

Layer 03

Tools registry

Allow-listed lookups (license registers, embassy contacts) called via Gemma 4’s native tool-call format.

Layer 04

Corridor adapters

Small LoRA adapters per corridor + sector, layered on top of the same base weights.

Layer 05

Evaluation suite

Reproducible eval packs — reviewers run the harness against pinned pack versions and replay outputs.

Layer 06

Safety harness

Refusal logic, citation enforcement, audit emission. Gemma 4 doesn’t do this on its own; the harness does.

04 · What this means in practice

Run DueCare on your laptop. On your phone. On your NGO’s tiny VM.

The same harness, the same packs, the same audit feed — on infrastructure you control.
