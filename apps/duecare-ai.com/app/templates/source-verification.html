Entity intelligence & official-source verification

Is this recruiter licensed? Is this employer flagged?

DueCare checks recruitment agencies, employers, and the companies behind them against official government registries — licensed-agency registers, debarment lists, and forced-labour registries. This is research tooling: it produces proposed findings for curator review, the same one-way public source → vetted pack lifecycle as our context packs. It is not a live worker-facing lookup, and no worker case is ever an input.

02 · How it works

Official registry → resolve → link → proposed finding.

Field maps are authored against the live registry response — never guessed. A source that blocks or returns nothing parseable is skipped and logged, never fabricated.

Public source step · 01

Official registry

A government licensed-agency register, debarment list, or forced-labour registry.

e.g. US CBP WRO · UK Modern Slavery
→
resolve
Deterministic step · 02

Resolve & normalise

Config-driven resolver fetches + parses the source into canonical entity records.

registry_spec · csv/json/html/pdf
→
link
Linkage step · 03

Link on the LEI

Probabilistic record-linkage joins the same company across registries on the GLEIF Legal Entity Identifier.

splink · GLEIF LEI · ownership edges
→
review
Human gate step · 04

Proposed finding

A curator reviews matches and sources before anything reaches a worker-facing surface.

propose-only · curator review

▸ Findings stage to a review queue, never the live model or a worker's screen directly. The lifecycle is one-way: public registry → reviewed finding.
